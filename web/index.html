    GameplayGravity and combinable clear rewards
    Piece selection

    Theorycrafting: set the next piece from the playfield. The current piece stays unchanged.

    Clearing

    A full middle slice needs all 9 cells, including the core. Surface-ring mode needs only its 8 surface cells. Either mode clears the entire slice. Each cleared face or slice grants the same score and perks.

    Gravity

    Off: clearing an interior ring or full slice causes no gravity. Face clears still follow the selected gravity mode.

    Each separated piece moves at most one cell when gravity runs. Another cascade clear can trigger another step. Off: settle until blocked.

    Clears only: fall toward the most recently cleared face or slice for that cascade, then stop. Always on: begins downward; each move has a 50% chance to keep its direction and a 50% chance to choose another face. Undo restores the random result.

    Floating fragments
    White cubie options

    Each cleared face grants one completed-piece turn. Broken pieces turn white and move with your active cubies, but do not count toward the target. White cubies must be clear of the fixed stack before they lock with your completed piece. Multi-clears and cascades add turns. Switching this off lets existing white cubies finish their current turn.

    Connected white cubies from the same original piece stay together. Turns that split their bandage are blocked. White groups retain their connected shape.

    Use Lock white pieces below the cube, then click a white group. Occupied stack cells block locking. Clears wait for normal piece placement; Undo restores the group.

    Each clear selects distinct cubies from the remaining stack. They turn white and relock with your next completed piece. Can be combined with broken fragments and reconstruction.

    Whole tetromino reconstruction

    Released whole pieces keep their colors. Same-colored rebuildable cubies can mix freely; form complete planar tetrominos from all of them. The current piece must use its own spheres. All must be valid, and clear of the stack, when your current piece locks. Rebuilding pieces are not bandaged. Whole pieces are selected first, then broken fragments, then random individual cubies; a cubie is never selected twice. One random whole piece per cleared plane is the default. These rewards can be combined independently. Disabling release modes leaves already freed cubies playable until they lock.

    How to play Rules & keyboard controls

    Build, then stack

    Turn the active segments until they form the target tetromino on a face. Centers are active cubies too: a center is usable only when your active center occupies it. It locks automatically when only one face fits. When multiple faces fit, choose a highlighted face button. Unused active segments disappear. A brief ghost marks the last placement. Active segments may pass through the fixed stack, but a placement needs four empty cells.

    Clear a whole face

    Fill all nine cells of a face to clear it. Full faces clear simultaneously. By default, connected fragments fall toward the last cleared face (ties use D, F, R, B, L, U order), then gravity switches off. Settings also offers no gravity or continuous gravity with random direction changes on moves.

    Every turn is available

    Face turns use U D F B L R. Middle slices use M E S. Hold Shift for the inverse, or Alt with a face key for a wide turn. M turns like R, E like D, S like F. A ✓ highlights any turn that immediately places your piece, including in other tabs.

    Gaps keep you alive

    Unfinished spawns include one active center plus edges and corners, spread across empty cells. If no unfinished spawn fits, the piece fills a facebound gap automatically. If no facebound gap fits, it tries a planar surface-only gap, then one through the core. Only failure of all three tiers ends the game. Automatic fills can clear faces and continue into the next piece. Undo restores the entire chain.
