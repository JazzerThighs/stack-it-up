How to play Rules & keyboard controls

Placement

Build the current piece on a face, or B/D/F around the core. Choose a highlighted placement when several fit. Active cubies may cross the stack; locking requires empty cells.

Clears

Matching planes clear together. Gravity and freed pieces follow your rules. Freed cubies lock when the current piece succeeds.

Keyboard

Face keys: U D F B L R. Slices: M E S. Shift reverses a key turn; The wide-turn button selects wide moves. C holds; Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes when allowed.

Spawning and top out

Planar pieces try face gaps, then surface gaps, then gaps through the core. B/D/F stay outside the core. If an unfinished spawn cannot fit, a valid gap may fill automatically. No valid placement means top out.
